Research

My work combines empirical measurement and evidence-based evaluation to make AI systems more reliable and more practical.

Research direction 01

Trustworthy and responsible language models

How can language models distinguish supported facts from fluent but incorrect answers? My research studies multilingual factuality and retrieval-augmented question answering.

EMNLP Findings 2026

Poly-FEVER

A factuality benchmark across 11 languages, designed to study language-sensitive hallucination detection.

CHASE 2025

HALO

Research direction 02

Agentic AI, MCP and LLM inference cost

Tool use introduces more than model inference time: schemas, planning, serialization, and answer synthesis all affect token consumption and latency. I investigate the costs of these interactions and opportunities for optimization.

ACL Findings 2026

ProMCP

Profiles the complete MCP workflow, with token-level and latency-level visibility across 20 servers and 169 tools.
