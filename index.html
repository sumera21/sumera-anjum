Welcome to my academic homepage

Hi, I’m Sumera.

I’m a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of North Texas, where I work with Dr. Yunhe Feng in the Responsible AI Lab. My research is about making language models more reliable—and understanding the computational cost of the systems we build around them.

Much of my work begins with a simple question: Does this actually work the way we expect? I’ve explored that question in multilingual fact verification, retrieval-augmented clinical question answering, tool-using LLM agents, and more recently quantum program generation.

In Fall 2026, I also joined Argonne National Laboratory as a visiting student, where I’m studying ways to reduce the cost of LLM-based workflows.

I enjoy working with students, sharing what I learn, and seeing a research idea turn into something people can actually use. You can find my papers below, or read a little more about me outside of research.
