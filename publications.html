Poly-FEVER: A Multilingual Hallucination Detection Benchmark

Hanzhi Zhang, Sumera Anjum, Heng Fan, Weijian Zheng, Yan Huang, Yunhe Feng

Findings of EMNLP 2026

Paper ↗Dataset ↗
Abstract

Poly-FEVER is a multilingual fact-verification benchmark containing 77,973 supported and refuted claims aligned across 11 languages. It evaluates how factuality judgments and hallucination detection vary across languages, model families, and prompting strategies.

ProMCP: Profiling Token Flows and Latency Costs in Model Context Protocol–Based LLM Agents

Sumera Anjum, Weijian Zheng, Rajkumar Kettimuthu, Heng Fan, Yunhe Feng

Findings of ACL 2026 · Oral Presentation

Paper ↗Code ↗
Abstract

ProMCP profiles token usage and latency throughout a six-stage Model Context Protocol pipeline, studying 20 MCP servers and 169 tools across local, cloud, and off-the-shelf agent configurations. It identifies orchestration and answer synthesis as significant cost drivers.

HALO: Hallucination Analysis and Learning Optimization to Empower LLMs with Retrieval-Augmented Context for Guided Clinical Decision Making

Sumera Anjum, Hanzhi Zhang, Wenjun Zhou, Eun Jin Paek, Xiaopeng Zhao, Yunhe Feng

IEEE/ACM CHASE 2025

Paper ↗Code ↗
Abstract

HALO combines external retrieval, query variation, and maximum marginal relevance to improve the reliability of large language models for clinical question answering. It is evaluated on MedMCQA and studies the effect of retrieved context on hallucinations.
