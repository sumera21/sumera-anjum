Beyond Research

A few things that make life interesting outside papers, experiments, and deadlines.

Creativity has always been part of my life

I love painting and henna art. They’re completely different from programming, but I enjoy the patience, detail, and experimentation involved in both. It’s nice to work on something where there isn’t always a single correct answer.

I also enjoy learning languages, visiting new places, and trying recipes in the kitchen. Traveling and meeting people from different backgrounds have shaped the way I look at the world.

Why I enjoy research

What I like most is the process of learning something unfamiliar, getting stuck, figuring out what I’m missing, and eventually seeing the pieces fit together. Some days that means reading and debugging; on others, it means explaining an idea to a student or talking through a problem with collaborators.

I hope this website reflects both sides of my life: the work I do as a researcher and the interests that keep me curious outside the lab.
