Toward AI worth trusting.

I am Sumera Anjum, a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of North Texas, advised by Dr. Yunhe Feng in the Responsible AI Lab. My work explores trustworthy language models, agentic AI and the Model Context Protocol (MCP), and generative AI for quantum computing.

I am also a Visiting Student in Data Science and Learning at Argonne National Laboratory, studying how to make LLM-based systems more resource-efficient.

ProMCP: Profiling Token Flows and Latency Costs in Model Context Protocol–Based LLM Agents

Sumera Anjum, Weijian Zheng, Rajkumar Kettimuthu, Heng Fan, Yunhe Feng

Findings of ACL 2026 · Oral Presentation

Paper ↗Code ↗
Abstract

ProMCP profiles token usage and latency throughout a six-stage Model Context Protocol pipeline, studying 20 MCP servers and 169 tools across local, cloud, and off-the-shelf agent configurations. It identifies orchestration and answer synthesis as significant cost drivers.

Poly-FEVER: A Multilingual Hallucination Detection Benchmark

Hanzhi Zhang, Sumera Anjum, Heng Fan, Weijian Zheng, Yan Huang, Yunhe Feng

Findings of EMNLP 2026

Paper ↗Dataset ↗
Abstract

Poly-FEVER is a multilingual fact-verification benchmark containing 77,973 supported and refuted claims aligned across 11 languages. It evaluates how factuality judgments and hallucination detection vary across languages, model families, and prompting strategies.

Beyond Vibe Coding: Unified Multi-Framework Quantum Code Generation and Execution-Aware Evaluation with LLMs

Sumera Anjum, Xiaokun Yang, Thomas Gerrity, Junhua Ding, Kewei Sha, Weijian Zheng, Song Fu, Antonio Paes, Yunhe Feng

IEEE International Conference on Quantum Artificial Intelligence (QAI 2026) · Accepted

Abstract

This work investigates quantum code generation with language models across PennyLane, Qiskit, and OpenQASM, using execution-aware evaluation to assess whether generated programs run and produce the intended quantum-state behavior.
