About Me

🎓Academic Pursuits

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of North Texas, advised by Dr. Yunhe Feng in the Responsible AI Lab. My research centers on 🛡️ Trustworthy LLMs, spanning the study of 🧠 AI Hallucination, 🤖 Agentic AI and the 🔌 Model Context Protocol (MCP), and ⚛️ Generative AI for Quantum Computing. Across all of it, I am working toward AI systems that are reliable, transparent, and accountable — dependable enough for the settings where mistakes carry real consequences.

📚Background

I completed my MS in Computer Science at UNT (2023–2025) with a 4.0 GPA, receiving the Distinguished Graduate Student Award, and my B.Tech in Computer Science at Sreenidhi Institute of Science and Technology in Hyderabad (2019–2023, GPA 8.91/10). Since 2023 I have worked as a Graduate Research Assistant in the Responsible AI Lab at UNT. Earlier, I was a Support Engineer Intern at Amazon (AWS) on the Kindle Reader team, where I built a self-service diagnostic tool on AWS Lambda, S3, and SNS that automated issue detection and root-cause analysis for the support pipeline.

✨Outside the Lab

If there is one thread running through my life, it is a joyful appetite for learning things from scratch. I speak 🗣️ Hindi, Urdu, Telugu, and English, and I'm currently adding Chinese and Arabic to the collection — because apparently studying how language models work all day wasn't enough languages for one person.

Away from the terminal, I pick up a brush instead of a keyboard: 🎨 painting and drawing intricate 🌿 henna (mehndi) designs are my favorite ways to think with my hands. I believe in collecting skills that make me more capable as a person, I learned to 🚗 drive and to 🏊 swim, and my current project is 🍳 cooking, which I'm learning one slightly-burnt experiment at a time.

I love ✈️ traveling and collecting new places the way I collect new languages. And after all of that, I am a fierce defender of my most underrated hobby: 😴 sleep, which I consider essential infrastructure for everything above.
